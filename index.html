    • Prologue

    Far to the East, thirteen people penetrated ancient ruins of the Imperial crypt, to absorb energy from the long-dead emperor. He ruled the entire human civilization on these lands having enormous witchcraft abilities. The leader of the intruders was Khan the Great, he opened the sarcophagus and got the main power, while all twelve of his followers shared remaining energy and received some powers.

    Ten years later, on the other side of the continent there was an unusually cold winter. It was especially hard time for people living in the northern regions. For example a small town Krevka, there lived a simple person called Yasen, it was me actually and I would like to tell my story. As I've already said the winter was freezing and the last month was just horrible with constant winds and blizzard, I didn't even see the light of the sun during these days. We spent two times more wood to warm our houses, plus food supplies were running low, the previous autumn gave us a poor harvest. I had a small house so warming it wasn't such an issue, but food was another story, in short only some stale bread left on my shelters. So I decided to visit local tavern, they sometimes offered some small job in exchange for a food. The tavern keeper - mister Olaf was an old bold guy, sometimes maybe a bit rude. That day he said that all work had already been done by others who came earlier, but then pointed me to some stranger in the far angle of the tavern, he had some job. So I approached the stranger, he was in black leather jacket, with a dark hair and beard, his name was quite unusual - Volk, meaning wolf in his native language. Volk shared some food with me and told about the deal in which he needed a help.

    The problem with wood was escalated by the local lord laws by which it was prohibited to not only hunt and cut the trees, but just to be in the nearest forest, which this lord made his own hunting grounds. The next forest in which citizens could get wood was inaccessible because the road was covered by a large layer of snow due to still continuing blizzard. Volk suggested to enter the lord's hunting grounds during the night and cut a few trees, that wood they could be exchanged for a food and money from Olaf. According to the tavern keeper Lord and all his guards would be at the feast in the castle, because of the lord's young son birthday party.

    Eventually, having weighed everything up, especially considering the situation with a food, I agreed for this business. Volk prepared a mule harnessed to a sleigh, two axes, two-man saw and we set off at night. There were no guards in the town, so we were sure in our success. In the forest we cut a few pine trees, but then to our misfortune appeared big hunting dogs, one tried to attack Volk and he hit her with Axe to death, I just jumped to the nearest tree. Right after dogs, horsemen arrived... They surrounded Volk with spears, then the lord himself approached. He shut at Volk about the cost of this dog, it was a very expensive rare west hound for which he spent a lot. After a moment of an anxious silence, treacherous branch snapped and I fell on the snow. Then lord ordered his soldiers to get us into prison to think what to do with us. We didn't really know if we were lucky that lord didn't kill us immediately or we should prepare for some worse execution.

    For our surprise we were kept in the prison until first days of spring. Fortunately upon overseers there were my old acquaintances, so imprisonment wasn't that bad, we were fed daily and it was warm enough, so I could say that we solved our problems with food and cold for some time! Although we still didn't know our fate, but seeing how they continued to keep us alive and healthy, we guessed that they are preparing us for a voyage to a slave market.
